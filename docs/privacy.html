AllDrivia privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Español

AllDrivia is a Windows application developed by Mateo Vilar ("the author") that mounts servers and cloud storage services as drives on your computer. This policy explains what data the application handles.

Summary

AllDrivia runs entirely on your computer. The author has no servers that receive your files, your credentials or usage data. There are no user accounts, ads, analytics or telemetry.

Data stored on your computer

The list of sites, their options and the access credentials (passwords or permissions granted to clouds) are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) for your account. Files you open are kept temporarily in a local cache. You can delete everything by removing the sites, or by uninstalling the application and deleting the %LOCALAPPDATA%\AllDrivia folder.

Google data

If you connect Google Drive, AllDrivia asks, with your consent, for permission to see, edit, create and delete your Google Drive files. It uses it only to show those files as a drive on your computer and to read, save, move and delete files when you do so from Windows. Data travels directly between your computer and Google; it is not sent to the author or to third parties, it is not used for advertising or to train artificial intelligence models, and nobody reads it but you.

AllDrivia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Other clouds and servers

The same applies to OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, S3, FTP, SFTP, WebDAV and other services: AllDrivia connects directly from your computer with the credentials you provide, only to show and handle your files.

Updates

Unless disabled, AllDrivia checks once a day for a new version by downloading a small file from GitHub (github.com). It sends no data about you; GitHub may log the request's IP address under its own privacy policy.

Licences

The licence is checked on your computer, offline. The licensee's name is part of the licence itself.

Children

AllDrivia is not directed at children and does not collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its date. For any question: mateovilar@gmail.com.
